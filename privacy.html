Privacy Policy

Number Tiles · Last updated: July 14, 2026

Number Tiles ("the app") is a puzzle game that runs entirely on your device. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following locally on your device (using your browser or device local storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Your current game progress (level, score, coins)
  • Your settings (such as sound and vibration preferences)

You can clear this data at any time by using the app's reset option or by clearing your browser/app storage.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any data. Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing to share.

Third-party services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the app's Microsoft Store listing support page.
